# Lab Group activity: Designing for your customer

| **120 MIN**  Session length | **2**  Scenario choices | **45 MIN**  Activity length |
| --- | --- | --- |

### Getting started

* Form a team of 4-6.
* Customers are assigned by logo on the table
* Name a facilitator, scribe, and spokesperson.
* Use the cheat sheet, each other, your own Control Hub for reference, and/or help.webex.com.

### What every team should deliver

* Prioritized customer pains and desired outcomes in the customer's language.
* A coherent set of Webex Calling features mapped to the numbered requirements.
* At least three discovery questions or clearly stated assumptions that could change the design.
* One recommendation based on your previous experience, i.e., something the customer may not have thought of.
* Prepare a 2-3 minute recommendation to share
